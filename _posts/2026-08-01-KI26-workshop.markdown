### Workshop at KI 2026

I’m excited to announce our workshop **“Explainable AI for Causal Reasoning”** at **KI 2026**.

The workshop explores how explainable AI and causal reasoning can be brought together to make AI systems more transparent, interpretable, and trustworthy.

More details and the full program are available on the [workshop website](https://gesina.github.io/ki2026-c-xai-tutorial/).
